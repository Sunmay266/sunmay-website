INTERNATIONAL PATIENTS

Welcome to Sunmay TCM Clinic, Taipei

Sunmay is an English-friendly TCM and acupuncture clinic in Taipei. We welcome international patients, expats, and travelers who are interested in experiencing Traditional Chinese Medicine in Taiwan.

Sunmay TCM Clinic is located in Taipei's Da'an District, right behind Cathay General Hospital and a short walk from Xinyi Anhe MRT Station. Our services include acupuncture and pain management — for neck and shoulder tension, back pain, muscle tightness, and sports-related discomfort — as well as facial acupuncture and wellness care.

We've welcomed a growing number of international visitors who come to Taipei specifically for self-pay treatments that are often more accessible here than at home, and that can show visible, meaningful results in just a few sessions.

Meet Dr. Tung

董芯羽 · Dr. Tung

TCM PHYSICIAN · SUNMAY TCM CLINIC

Dr. Tung graduated from the Department of Chinese Medicine at China Medical University, with a minor in Sports Medicine. Her practice spans internal medicine, women's and pediatric health, musculoskeletal/pain medicine, and sports medicine — she looks for the root cause in a patient's lifestyle and body structure rather than only treating symptoms.

In women's and pediatric care, she focuses on menstrual irregularities, PMS, fertility preparation, and pediatric growth. In musculoskeletal medicine, she treats myofascial pain, degenerative joint conditions, and facial nerve palsy, combining her sports medicine background with movement and posture assessment.

In aesthetic medicine, Dr. Tung is a FACE-certified facial acupuncture physician, using facial acupuncture, micro-needling, and acupoint embedding to help patients improve both their physique and complexion from within.

Treatments Popular with International Visitors

Facial Acupuncture

美顏針

Ultra-fine needles stimulate facial meridians and muscles to naturally lift contours, soften fine lines, and improve circulation — no injections, no downtime. Performed by Dr. Tung, a FACE-certified facial acupuncture physician.

Acupotomy

超微針刀・小針刀・圓針

A minimally invasive technique that releases deep tissue adhesions for chronic pain (frozen shoulder, cervical/lumbar conditions, sciatica, knee degeneration), guided by ultrasound and posture analysis. Noticeable change often within 1-3 sessions.

Fu's Subcutaneous Needling (FSN)

浮針療法

A low-pain, minimally invasive technique that releases superficial fascial tension — effective for stiff neck, plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, and migraines. Fast-acting, usually 3-6 sessions, well suited to needle-shy patients.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept international or tourist patients?

Yes. We regularly welcome international visitors to Taipei, including patients seeking high-value self-pay treatments such as facial acupuncture, acupotomy, Fu's subcutaneous needling, and TCM weight loss. We recommend booking in advance via LINE so we can prepare for your visit.

Can I get a receipt or medical certificate for my overseas insurance claim?

Yes. For international patients who need documents for overseas insurance claims, we can provide medical receipts and relevant documentation upon request. Reimbursement depends on your individual insurance policy and provider — we recommend confirming your insurer's required format with us before your visit.

Do the doctors speak English?

English consultations are available with our English-speaking TCM doctor, Dr. Tung. For appointments with our other physician, we recommend bringing a translator or using a translation app to ensure clear communication.

What are Dr. Tung's clinic hours?

Dr. Tung sees patients: Tuesday 14:00–21:00, Wednesday 10:00–17:30, Thursday 10:00–13:30, and Saturday 13:30–17:00 (Taipei time). Check-in closes 15 minutes before each session ends, and hours may be adjusted — we recommend booking in advance via LINE to confirm availability for your travel dates.

Can I complete a treatment course during a short trip to Taipei?

It depends on the treatment. Acupotomy and Fu's subcutaneous needling often show noticeable results within 1-3 sessions, and facial acupuncture can be scheduled across consecutive visits. Our doctors will assess your condition and suggest a visit schedule that fits your travel dates.

How do I book an appointment as an international patient?

The easiest way is to message our LINE official account (@068ohdvi) with your preferred dates, the treatment you're interested in, and any insurance documentation needs. You can also call +886-2-2701-0768.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash (NTD) and major credit cards for self-pay treatments. Please note that insurance reimbursement is processed by your own insurance provider after your visit, not directly by our clinic.
