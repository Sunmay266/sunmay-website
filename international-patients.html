INTERNATIONAL PATIENTS

Welcome to Sunmay TCM Clinic (森美中醫診所), Taipei

Sunmay is an English-friendly TCM and acupuncture clinic in Taipei. We welcome international patients, expats, and travelers who are interested in experiencing Traditional Chinese Medicine in Taiwan.

Sunmay TCM Clinic is located in Taipei's Da'an District, right behind Cathay General Hospital and a short walk from Xinyi Anhe MRT Station. Our services include acupuncture and pain management — for neck and shoulder tension, back pain, muscle tightness, and sports-related discomfort — as well as facial acupuncture and wellness care.

Whether you're visiting Taipei for a short trip or living and working here as an expat, Sunmay is a welcoming place to experience Traditional Chinese Medicine. Many of our treatments are designed to show visible, meaningful results in just a few sessions, and we're happy to plan a schedule around your travel dates — or, if you're based in Taipei, around ongoing care that fits your routine.

Meet Dr. Tung

董芯羽 · Dr. Tung

TCM PHYSICIAN · SUNMAY TCM CLINIC

Dr. Tung graduated from the Department of Chinese Medicine at China Medical University, with a minor in Sports Medicine. Her practice focuses on internal medicine, women's and pediatric health, musculoskeletal pain, and sports medicine.

Rather than treating symptoms alone, Dr. Tung takes a whole-person approach, looking at factors such as lifestyle, posture, movement patterns, and physical function to better understand what may be contributing to a patient's condition.

In women's and pediatric care, she focuses on menstrual irregularities, PMS, fertility and preparing concerns, and pediatric growth and development. Learn more on our gynecology treatment page (Chinese only).

For musculoskeletal and pain conditions, Dr. Tung treats myofascial pain, degenerative joint problems, and facial palsy. With her background in sports medicine, she also incorporates posture, movement, and range-of-motion assessment into her clinical evaluation as needed.

Dr. Tung also provides aesthetic acupuncture treatments, including facial acupuncture and micro-needling for facial and skin concerns, as well as thread-embedding acupuncture for weight management and body contouring (Chinese only). Her approach focuses on supporting a natural appearance and overall well-being through individualized treatment.

Treatments Popular with International Visitors

Facial Acupuncture

美顏針

Ultra-fine needles stimulate facial meridians and muscles to naturally lift contours, soften fine lines, and improve circulation — no injections, no downtime. Performed by Dr. Tung, a FACE-certified facial acupuncture physician.

Acupotomy

超微針刀・小針刀・圓針

A minimally invasive technique that releases deep tissue adhesions for chronic pain (frozen shoulder, cervical/lumbar conditions, sciatica, knee degeneration), guided by ultrasound and posture analysis. Noticeable change often within 1-3 sessions.

Fu's Subcutaneous Needling (FSN)

浮針療法

A low-pain, minimally invasive technique that releases superficial fascial tension — effective for stiff neck, plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, and migraines. Fast-acting, usually 3-6 sessions, well suited to needle-shy patients.

Can I complete a treatment course during a short trip to Taipei? +

It depends on the treatment. Acupotomy and Fu's subcutaneous needling often show noticeable results within 1-3 sessions, and facial acupuncture can be scheduled across consecutive visits. Our doctors will assess your condition and suggest a visit schedule that fits your travel dates. If you live and work in Taipei, we're also happy to plan ongoing care around your regular schedule rather than a single trip.

Can herbal formulas be made vegetarian? +

Yes. Many of our herbal prescriptions can be adjusted to avoid animal-derived ingredients. Just let us know your dietary preference when you book or during your consultation, and our doctors will plan your formula accordingly.

Can I get a longer supply of herbal medicine to take home with me? +

Yes. Depending on your condition, Dr. Tung can prescribe herbal medicine for anywhere from one week up to three months, so you can continue your treatment after you return home. If you need supporting documentation for customs or airport requirements when traveling with your medication, just let us know in advance and we can prepare it.

How do I book an appointment as an international patient? +

The easiest way is to message our LINE official account (@068ohdvi) with your preferred dates, the treatment you're interested in, and any insurance documentation needs. You can also email us at sunmay1520@gmail.com or call +886-2-2701-0768.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept cash (NTD) and bank transfer to a Taiwan bank account for self-pay treatments. We do not accept credit cards. Please note that insurance reimbursement is processed by your own insurance provider after your visit, not directly by our clinic.
